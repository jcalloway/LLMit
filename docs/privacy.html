Privacy Policy

LLMit — AI Usage Tracker

Last updated 7 September 2026

LLMit runs entirely on your device. It has no server, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your data never leaves your phone except to fetch your usage from the AI services you sign into.

What stays on your device

All of this is stored locally and never sent anywhere:

  • App preferences (theme, widget layout, background opacity)
  • Cached usage data displayed in widgets
  • Notification settings and thresholds

Authentication cookies

When you sign in to an AI service (Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor), LLMit stores the session cookie on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences. This cookie is used only to fetch your usage data from that service's website. It is never sent to any other server, and LLMit has no server of its own to send it to.

Network requests

LLMit connects only to the AI services you have signed into:

  • Claude: claude.ai (Anthropic)
  • ChatGPT: api.openai.com and chatgpt.com (OpenAI)
  • Cursor: www.cursor.com (Anysphere)

These requests fetch your subscription usage data. No other network connections are made. LLMit does not contact any analytics service, ad network, or third-party server.

What we never collect

  • Names, email addresses, or accounts of any kind
  • Location data
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data
  • Contacts, call logs, or any other personal information
  • The content of your conversations with any AI service
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

Third parties

LLMit does not share any data with third parties. The only network traffic is between your device and the AI services you choose to sign into, using those services' own websites.

Deleting your data

To remove all data stored by LLMit, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. There is no server-side data to delete.

Children

LLMit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes
