# Google Play Store Listing — LLMit

## Short description (80 chars max)
Track your Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor usage limits from your home screen.

## Full description (4000 chars max)
LLMit puts your AI subscription usage right on your Android home screen. Glance at a widget to see how much of your Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor plan you've used — no need to open the browser.

WIDGETS FOR EVERY STYLE
• Gauge — a single-service radial meter
• Bars — side-by-side usage bars for all your services
• Dashboard — a detailed breakdown with model-level stats

SERVICES SUPPORTED
• Claude (Anthropic) — 5-hour and weekly limits, including Fable usage
• ChatGPT (OpenAI) — GPT-4o and o-series limits
• Cursor — fast and slow request counts

NEAR-LIMIT NOTIFICATIONS
Get notified when you're approaching your usage cap so you can pace yourself or switch models.

PRIVACY FIRST
LLMit has no server, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your session cookies are stored in Android's encrypted storage. The app connects only to the AI services you sign into — nothing else.

CUSTOMIZABLE
• Adjustable background opacity to blend with any wallpaper
• Light and dark themes
• Multiple widget sizes and layouts
• Per-service accent colors
